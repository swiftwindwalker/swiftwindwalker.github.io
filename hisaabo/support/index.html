Hisaabo brings your accounts, card spending, budgets and goals into one view. Here are answers to the things that most often need a second look.

Why does Hisaabo show an advertisement?

The free version uses Google AdMob advertising to support development. Advertisements are labelled and separate from your financial records. Where required, manage consent through Settings → Advertising privacy. Financial records are not supplied to the ad service.

How do I get started?

Add your bank, cash and credit-card accounts in Settings. Enter opening amounts from before your first tracked transaction. Set a billing day and credit limit for each credit card. Link debit-card and UPI methods to their bank, then start recording transactions.

Why can’t I add a credit-card expense?

The expense may exceed the card’s credit limit, or the limit may not be configured. Check the account details. Record a repayment as a bank-to-card transfer, or update the limit if it has changed. Future-dated and edited records can also affect the limit checks.

Why are my card cycle and monthly spending different?

Calendar-month reports combine all accounts for the selected month. Each credit card also has its own statement cycle. A closing day of the 15th covers the 16th of the previous month through the 15th. Cycle purchases are not the same as a bank statement or payment amount due.

How do I record a credit-card repayment?

Use Transfer: choose your bank as From and the credit card as To. Recording the repayment as another expense would count the spending twice.

Can I back up or move my records?

In Settings, export a JSON backup and keep a copy you can access. Restore using Import JSON backup, or paste a valid backup through Import JSON text. Import replaces the current ledger; export a fresh backup first. CSV is for viewing transactions and cannot restore the complete app.

Will my data survive deleting the app or losing my phone?

Do not rely on automatic device backup for the financial ledger: it is marked excluded from system backups. Deleting the app or losing your device can permanently lose records unless you have a manual JSON export. A backup on the same lost device does not protect against device loss.

Does changing currency convert my money?

No. Hisaabo uses one currency for the ledger. Changing it changes currency labels while keeping numeric amounts unchanged. It does not perform foreign-exchange conversion.

The email button doesn’t open anything. What should I do?

Use Copy email address, then paste it into your preferred email app or webmail. An email link needs a compatible email app; it may not work in an iPhone simulator.

What should I include in a bug report?

Include your iPhone model, iOS version, Hisaabo version, the steps to reproduce the issue, and what you expected to happen. Share a redacted screenshot if useful. Please do not send PINs, passwords, security codes, full card numbers or an unredacted financial backup.

Before you troubleshoot

Export a JSON backup before reinstalling, resetting, or importing data. Do not delete the app to fix an issue if it holds your only copy of important records.

A note on your numbers

Hisaabo tracks records you enter. It does not connect to your bank, move money, or replace your bank statements.
